Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

At ThumbPreview, your privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Introduction

ThumbPreview ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website located at public-nine-swart.vercel.app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Service. By using ThumbPreview, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Image Processing and Privacy

ThumbPreview is designed with a privacy-first architecture. When you upload a thumbnail image to our tool, the image is processed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript APIs (specifically, URL.createObjectURL). Your image is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party server. No image data is stored, cached, logged, or accessible to us in any way.

When you close the browser tab, navigate away from the page, or click the Reset button, the temporary local reference to your image is automatically revoked and destroyed. We have no ability to recover, view, or access any image you use with our tool.

3. Information We Collect

While your images remain completely private, we may collect the following anonymous, non-personal information when you use the Preview feature:

  • Thumbnail filename — The name of the file you uploaded (not the image itself).
  • Video title and channel name — The text you enter in the customization fields.
  • User-Agent string — Your browser type and version.
  • Screen resolution — The dimensions of your display.
  • Referrer URL — The website that directed you to ThumbPreview.

This data is collected solely for the purpose of understanding how the tool is used and improving the Service. It does not include any personally identifiable information and cannot be used to identify individual users.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

ThumbPreview uses the following browser storage mechanisms:

  • localStorage — We store your theme preference (light mode or dark mode) so that it persists across visits. This contains only the string "light" or "dark" and no personal data.
  • sessionStorage — We use session storage to prevent duplicate visitor counting within the same browsing session.
  • Third-party cookies — Our advertising partners (see Section 5) may set cookies on your device to serve relevant advertisements and measure ad performance. These cookies are governed by the respective third party's privacy policy.

5. Third-Party Services

ThumbPreview uses the following third-party services:

  • Adsterra — We display advertisements provided by Adsterra to support the free operation of this tool. Adsterra may use cookies and tracking technologies to serve ads. You can review Adsterra's privacy policy at their website.
  • Google Fonts — We load typefaces from Google Fonts to render our user interface. Google may log font requests. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com.
  • counterapi.dev — We use this free API to maintain anonymous visitor counts (today's visitors and total visitors) displayed in the footer. No personal information is sent to this service.
  • Vercel — Our website is hosted on Vercel's free tier. Vercel may collect standard web server logs including IP addresses and request timestamps. You can review Vercel's privacy policy at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous usage data collected via our webhook is retained indefinitely for aggregate statistical analysis. Because this data contains no personally identifiable information, it cannot be linked to individual users. We do not maintain user accounts, profiles, or any form of persistent user identification.

7. Children's Privacy

ThumbPreview is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Because ThumbPreview does not collect personally identifiable information, there is generally no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you have concerns about any data associated with your use of the Service, please contact us and we will address your inquiry promptly.

If you wish to prevent third-party advertising cookies, you can adjust your browser settings to block third-party cookies or use browser extensions designed for ad and tracker blocking.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through our GitHub repository at github.com/taeshin11/thumbpreview.
